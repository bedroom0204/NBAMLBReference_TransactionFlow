Installation Steps in Google Chrome:

  1. Open Chrome and navigate to chrome://extensions/
  2. Toggle ON Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  3. Click Load unpacked in the top-left corner.
  4. Select the directory: C:\UX\AI\ExtensionforTransaction
  5. Done! Visit any player page on Basketball-Reference or Baseball-Reference (e.g. Josh Green Transactions) to view the automated visual timeline!
